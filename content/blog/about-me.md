I lead the design system team at [7shifts](https://www.7shifts.com/), where I created [Sous Chef](https://sous-chef.7shifts.software/). It started as a fragmented set of components and is now a themeable React system of 100+ components. These days most of my time goes into a newer problem: AI agents write a lot of our UI code, and I want them to reach for Sous Chef the same way our engineers do.

I live in [Saskatoon, Canada, the land of living skies](https://www.instagram.com/visitsaskatoon/). I'm originally from Brazil and moved here in 2019 to join 7shifts, which builds team management software for restaurants.

![Me at the heart of Saskatoon](/assets/personal-image-2.png 'Me at the heart of Saskatoon')

## What I care about

Most of my work is the layer underneath features: the components, tokens and tooling that other engineers build on. Three things keep coming up.

**Design systems.** Sous Chef is the shared language between design and engineering at 7shifts. I planned it and built it from the first component.

**Developer experience.** I like building pits of success, where the easy way is also the right way. If a component isn't intuitive, accessible and fast, it isn't done yet.

**Testing.** I follow the Testing Trophy: tests should check what users do with your code, not how you wrote it.

## How I got here

I've been building software for more than 15 years.

Before Canada, I spent four years at [TOTVS](https://en.totvs.com/), the largest tech company in Latin America. Working on software used by thousands of customers taught me how much discipline maintenance takes.

At 7shifts and Living Skies Technologies I moved into frontend. I led migrations from legacy PHP to React single-page apps, and spent a good amount of time paying down technical debt.

Today I lead a design system team. We work on semantic color tokens, dark mode, and the tooling that lets AI agents use the system.

## Building Sous Chef

In 2022 I joined a new Developer Experience team at 7shifts. The mission was to take the friction out of frontend work, and for me that meant Sous Chef.

I built its foundation with React, TypeScript and Storybook, and added Chromatic to our CI so every visual change across the 100+ components is reviewed before it ships. Sous Chef is now the single source of truth for our UI, and the work grew into a dedicated design system team, which I lead.
