I lead the design system team at [7shifts](https://www.7shifts.com/), where I created [Sous Chef](https://sous-chef.7shifts.software/): a React design system that grew from a fragmented set of components into a themeable system of 100+ components. Today my focus is teaching AI agents to build with it instead of around it.

I live in [Saskatoon, Canada, the land of living skies](https://www.instagram.com/visitsaskatoon/). Originally from Brazil, I moved here in 2019 to join 7shifts, where we build team management software that helps restaurant managers reclaim their time.

![Me at the heart of Saskatoon](/assets/personal-image-2.png 'Me at the heart of Saskatoon')

## What I do

I sit at the intersection of high-fidelity UI and scalable frontend architecture. I don't just build features; I build the systems that make building features effortless.

- **Design systems**: I am the founding architect of Sous Chef, the design system that unified design and engineering at 7shifts.
- **Developer experience**: I'm obsessed with creating pit of success workflows. If a tool or component isn't intuitive, accessible and fast, it isn't finished.
- **Quality and confidence**: I am a staunch advocate for the Testing Trophy. Tests should reflect how users interact with your code, not how you implemented it.

## My journey

My career spans more than **15 years**, from the enterprise ERP landscape of Latin America to Canadian startups.

- **The enterprise era**: Before Canada, I spent 4 years at [TOTVS](https://en.totvs.com/), the largest tech company in Latin America. There I learned the discipline it takes to maintain software used by thousands of customers.
- **The startup pivot**: At 7shifts and Living Skies Technologies, I became a frontend specialist, leading migrations from legacy PHP to modern React SPAs and rescuing architectures from technical debt.
- **Today**: I lead a dedicated **design system team**, working on semantic color tokens, dark mode and **AI-driven automation**.

## Building Sous Chef

In 2022 I joined a newly formed Developer Experience team at 7shifts with a clear mission: remove friction from the frontend development lifecycle. My main focus was the architecture and maturity of Sous Chef, which is now the single source of truth for our UI.

- **Foundational architecture**: I led the planning and execution of the system from the ground up, establishing the core infrastructure with React, TypeScript and Storybook.
- **Visual confidence**: I integrated Chromatic into our CI pipelines to automate visual regression testing, so every change across our 100+ components is intentional.
- **Systemic impact**: The work on the DX team led to the creation of a dedicated design system team, where I now focus on craft and the system's evolution.
